# Front-view ukulele and strumming hand

Project assets:
- `/workspace/sites/uke-strum-drew/dist/assets/collage/strumming-ukulele-front.png`
- `/workspace/sites/uke-strum-drew/dist/assets/collage/strumming-hand-front.png`

Both illustrations were created with the built-in image-generation tool and retain transparent alpha channels. The instrument is mirrored in CSS so the body is on the left and the neck points right, as Drew requested for the audience's view. The separate hand is reflected vertically about its wrist in CSS to show a right hand with the thumb pointing up. It points right with its back toward the viewer and its fingers over the soundhole. The accepted timing curve is unchanged; only the wrist pivot, placement, scale, and direction of rotation changed to suit the front view. The illustration stays between 210 and 280 pixels wide, below the beat numbers.

## Ukulele generation prompt

Use case: illustration-story. Asset type: small front-facing ukulele sprite for a children's music teaching app. ONE complete soprano ukulele seen exactly from the FRONT: flat soundboard facing viewer, horizontal strings, headstock and neck extending to the LEFT, round double-lobed ukulele BODY on the RIGHT. Four strings, four tuning pegs, warm golden honey wood with believable grain, dark circular soundhole in the right-hand body, dark bridge to its right. Classic ukulele proportions, accurately recognizable, no guitar six-string details. Fully visible whole instrument with generous transparent padding. Horizontal neck centered on canvas mid-height, instrument takes almost full canvas width; landscape image. Saturated hand-painted gouache and subtle cut-paper collage style, organic realistic outline, warm painted shadows on the wood, no geometric icon look. Clean isolated silhouette, genuinely transparent alpha outside, opaque painted instrument. No person, NO HAND, no stand, no lettering, no background, no glow or surrounding shadows.

## Hand generation prompt

Use case: illustration-story. Asset type: a separate transparent hand sprite to overlay a FRONT-VIEW ukulele in a children's classroom app. One relaxed human hand, back of hand facing viewer, palm facing AWAY toward an imaginary flat instrument. Short wrist enters from LEFT, four fingers point toward the RIGHT, close together and gently curved downward in a relaxed down-strumming gesture. Fingers are extended softly as a group, not curled into a grasp. Thumb rests naturally along the lower edge of palm, no pinching, no ring-shaped opening, no fist, no holding pose. Anatomically correct five fingers, clear simple silhouette. A small turquoise cuff at left wrist, warm medium tan skin, saturated hand-painted gouache and subtle cut-paper collage texture. Realistic proportions with soft cartoon rendering, organic painted contours. This is the FLAT FRONT VIEW of the back of a strumming hand, NOT the side view. Entire isolated hand and cuff inside a landscape canvas with padding, fingertips face RIGHT, no arm or body. Genuine transparent alpha outside crisp silhouette, fully opaque hand. No instrument, no strings, no background, no glow, no surrounding shadow, no text.

## Verification

Behavioral checks cover stroke timing, selected patterns, swing, count-in, repeats, final strum, pause, student display, reduced motion, and both portable-player image payloads. Sprite bounds and string crossings are checked geometrically. Browser rendering and audio listening are unavailable in this environment.
