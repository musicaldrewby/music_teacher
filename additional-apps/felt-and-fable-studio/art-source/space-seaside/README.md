# Space Expedition and Seaside Adventure artwork

18 assets generated with the built-in image generation tool: 10 landscape backgrounds and 8 transparent props/characters.

- `manifest.json`: final asset names, themes, distribution paths, dimensions, and exact prompts.
- `prompts.json`: generation specifications.
- `generated.json`: original tool output paths recorded for provenance.
- `*.png`: original generated images, preserved without background erasure.
- `alpha-inspection.jpg`: visual check of all eight final WebP pieces composited over dark green; confirms clean transparent silhouettes.
- Distribution files: `art-source/cinema/space-seaside/*.webp`, encoded at quality 91 with original resolution and alpha preserved.

All backgrounds were inspected after generation. They use tactile felt miniature sets, clear playable floors, no people or lettering, and coherent colors within each pack. All props have real RGBA transparency; no painted checkerboard or removal masks were used.
