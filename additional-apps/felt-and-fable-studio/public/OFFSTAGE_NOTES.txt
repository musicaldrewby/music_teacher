FELT & FABLE v1.27 — OFFSTAGE WORKSPACE

A separate editor view shows and edits objects outside the camera frame.
Gold outline identifies the video shot; backdrop keeps its original world bounds.
Find selected, Fit all, an offscreen-piece list, workspace zoom and pan, and
automatic recovery after an offscreen drag keep pieces accessible.
Custom path drawing uses the same editor/world coordinate conversion and can
cross from one side of the shot to the other. Layers can locate remote pieces.
Only the editing canvas uses this view; scene cameras and saved story data are
unaffected by workspace navigation. Hidden/transparent selection ghosts, labels,
the frame outline and margins are not included in PNG, MP4 or presentation.

Validation used native canvas plus DOM stubs and actual registered pointer
callbacks: offscreen dragging and return, hit testing, recovery through Layers,
Fit all bounds, zoom around cursor, pan without changing story/camera, path
creation from offstage across the shot, and clipping parked pieces from output.
Output frames match the normal renderer at 30 and 60 fps with Offstage enabled.
All prior animation/path tests pass. Script syntax, HTML IDs/references, ZIP
integrity, update merge and unchanged artwork are also checked.

No browser executable was available for full interactive layout testing or an
actual MP4 encoding run. Native canvas checks are not browser usability tests.
