FELT & FABLE v1.24 — FAST START

WHAT CHANGED
- Full-quality artwork loads on demand in a queue of at most three loads.
- Collection browsing uses a separate lightweight thumbnail sheet.
- All 227 pieces and 65 backdrops remain available offline.
- Cutout cleanup and trimming are performed ahead of time, preserving the
  dimensions produced by v1.23 so existing saved stories keep their proportions.
- Full-bleed backgrounds retain their original encoded artwork, without another
  lossy compression pass. Prepared cutouts use lossless WebP.
- Scene loading displays progress. Repeated asset requests share one load.
- Story playback and both MP4 export modes prepare all story artwork first.
- PNG export waits for the selected scene's full-quality artwork.

MEASUREMENTS
v1.23 startup JavaScript data: 136,512,486 bytes.
v1.24 startup scripts plus opening-board artwork: 3,869,596 bytes.
Reduction: 97.2%. This measures bytes read, not a guaranteed loading-time reduction.
Initial full-size images: 7 instead of 292.

VALIDATION
All script syntax and references checked.
Native-canvas/DOM-stub tests: boot, full catalog browsing without extra originals,
School Day filters and search, lazy piece/backdrop selection, grouped saved-story
opening, custom grouped pieces, recursive multi-scene artwork preparation,
duplicate load suppression, missing-asset rejection, and a 1920x1080 scene render.
Native-canvas startup measured about 0.3 seconds in this test environment.
Browser startup depends on the device and browser; no real-browser timing or
actual MP4 encoding benchmark was performed in this environment.
Camera, audio, animation and encoding implementations remain in place.

INSTALL
Extract the complete v1.24 ZIP into a new folder, open index.html, and use
File > Open story for your existing saved story JSON files.
Keep the older app folder as a backup until you are happy with this version.
