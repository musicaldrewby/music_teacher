### Final atlas spacing adjustment

The two alternative atlases were refined with this image-generation edit prompt before integration:

Precise layout correction for this transparent sprite atlas. Preserve the exact eight painted/paper illustrations, their materials, gestures, and colors. Make each illustration SMALLER and add substantially more empty transparent space between them. Output exactly 2:1 landscape, four columns, two rows. Every cell is square. ALL pixels of each illustration, including impact marks and clothing, must fit inside the INNER 68% of its square cell. Thus each cell has a fully empty transparent border 16% of cell width on every side. Center each illustration at x=12.5%,37.5%,62.5%,87.5%, y=25% and75% of the canvas. The top row must have NO pixels below y=42% of canvas; bottom row NO pixels above y=58%. Leave a completely empty wide horizontal transparent gutter between rows. Keep generous vertical gutters too. No added words, lines, shapes, cell backgrounds, or frames. Do not enlarge individual drawings to fill the canvas. This correction is essential: eight isolated small illustrations, same actions and same order, substantial transparent empty gutters.
