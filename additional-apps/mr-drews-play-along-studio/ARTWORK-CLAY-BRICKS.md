# Claymation and brick artwork — October 11, 2026

Created with the built-in `image_gen.imagegen` tool, one call for each of seven original assets, with no reference images. Generated PNGs were copied unchanged into the app. This is a visual-art addition; the audio, rhythm timing and video export remain on the existing shared paths.

## Collections

- Three claymation worlds: Garden, Seaside, and Moon.
- One Claymation Puppets movement collection.
- Two brick-built worlds: Brick City and Brick Space.
- One LEGO-style Bricks movement collection.

All worlds and movement styles can be mixed independently under Look. Each atlas contains eight gestures in four columns and two rows: pat, clap, stomp, snap / say, shoulder tap, wave, freeze. Repeated pictures and rests are composed by the app for straight pairs, swung pairs, and compound patterns. No lettering is baked into the artwork, including the speech bubbles.

The background PNGs are 1672 × 941 RGB. Both movement PNGs are 1774 × 887 RGBA with original alpha preserved. Source windows in `catalog.mjs` preserve each gesture and avoid neighboring marks: the claymation atlas uses `NEW_ART_WINDOWS`; the bricks atlas additionally widens the speech-bubble window and shifts the shoulder-tap window right. The PNG pixels were not edited. The original Handmade Clay collection remains available.
