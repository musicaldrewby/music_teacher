Make yourself at home

TouchSlide the left stick to walk. Swipe the world to look. Tap Jump or hold it to swim up, hold Mine to break, and tap Use or Place at the crosshair. Rotate appears for pieces that can turn. Double-tap Jump to fly in Free build and hold Down to descend.

MouseMove to look around. Click Enter or Resume once to capture the mouse. Escape frees the cursor so you can click the top menus. Click the world or Resume in the top bar to return.

W A S DWalk. Hold Ctrl to sprint. Hold Shift to sneak and stay at ledges.

LMB / RMBHold left-click to break. Cracks build until the block breaks; releasing or changing targets resets them. Right-click uses instruments, opens doors, or places blocks.

ToolsIn Discovery, hold Mine or left-click to break a tree trunk with your fist. Walk over the little log to collect it. In Backpack → Craft, turn any log into four boards and two boards into four sticks. Craft a wooden pickaxe, axe, or shovel. Shape stone upgrades at the cabin bench or build a bench with four boards. A stone pickaxe opens buried instrument cases.

0Switch to your fist. Number keys 1–9 return to a hotbar item. Fist is also in Backpack → Tools.

TCarry a torch while using a tool. Select a torch in the hotbar and right-click a floor or wall to place it. Craft more in your backpack.

EOpen / close the backpack. B also opens it.

Space × 2Double-tap to fly in Free build. Space rises; Shift descends. Double-tap again to land.

SpaceJump on land or swim up in water. To climb out, move toward a one-block bank and press Space. Shift dives. You can stay underwater as long as you like.

1 – 9 / WheelChoose a hotbar slot. Middle-click picks the block you are looking at.

JOpen your explorer map. Mark a visited place or track the route home.

Sky & weatherWorld menu → Sky & weather adjusts the day length, freezes time, or chooses the weather. Use a bed to rest until morning.

M / ROpen the music studio / rotate furniture.

Cabin piecesFind roofs, beams, shutters and signs in Backpack → Building. Craft them in Discovery. Use a shutter to swing it open, or use a sign to paint and letter it.

RailwayBackpack → Railway supplies straight track, bends, slopes, switches and a cart. Place on solid ground, or aim at a rail end to extend it. R rotates. Use a switch to change its branch.

RidePlace a minecart on a rail and use it to board. Hold W or S to travel, release to brake, and press F or Space to get out. On touch, slide the stick forward or back and tap Use to get out. Set a station record on a nearby record player for your arrival.

Field notesLook for blue mesas, timber lookouts and broad cottonwoods on the horizon. Rain pools on level ground, snow dusts pines, and soft ground holds your footprints for a while.

Ctrl ZUndo your latest building or gathering action.

G / P / FHold G to break. P places; F uses. V also toggles flight.

Nearby terrain loads as you explore. Lower View distance in the world menu for a lighter experience. The same seed recreates the same landscape.

Start Discovery empty-handed, with one empty track. Open the brass case near your cabin to find your first instrument. Open cases and dig up violet inlays to discover individual sounds, mixers, track expansions, reverb, and echo. Open Collection to hear your sounds, expand tracks, and trade materials. Free build gives you unlimited building materials. Instruments still come from discoveries.
