In the studio

Open Shows ↗. Choose a journey, add movements, capture a studio sound, or open a show file. Select a movement to change its length, crossfade and lighting. Timed moves shape individual layers. Save show keeps the complete editable score.

WAV export renders audio offline. Record video captures live playback, with the file format chosen from the browser's supported MP4 or WebM options. Keep the tab visible. Long recordings use direct disk saving where available; browsers without it are limited to five-minute downloads.

Give the music a world

Event Horizon bends light around a dark star. Aurora Veils opens into flowing ribbons. Prism Garden lights up a field of resonant glass. Liquid Echoes ripples like illuminated water. Resonance Field braids particles into a living sculpture. Wave Cathedral builds flowing arches from the rhythm. Select a movement to choose its world, palette, camera, framing and glow. Music drives note pulses and reverb trails; movements dissolve into one another.

Try the visual tour loads all six in a two-minute show. Cinema removes every text overlay from the picture and recording. Choose it before playback. Fullscreen expands the picture; move your pointer or press Tab for transport controls, and Escape to return. Camera Still fixes the view; Visual motion at zero freezes the scene.

For an agent-made film

Use the renderer in Orbit's source checkout, or download the files listed in the manifest into their indicated paths. It requires Node.js and FFmpeg; video also uses skia-canvas. This is a local rendering workflow, with progress and resumable sections.

node scripts/show.mjs create --minutes 60 \
  --title "Beyond the Event Horizon" --out show.json
node scripts/render-show.mjs show.json \
  --out renders/horizon --width 2560 --height 1440 --fps 60 --crf 16

Offline films default to native 1440p at 60 frames per second with high-quality encoding, rendering every frame from the planned performance. For 4K, use --width 3840 --height 2160. The output includes the MP4 film, stereo WAV, editable show, poster and quality report. Render short transition previews first. Repeating the identical command resumes verified sections; previous audio is replayed to restore the continuing voices and effects.

Tell your agent the musical direction

“Make an hour of Event Horizon-style music. Begin quietly, introduce subtle rhythmic passages in the middle, and finish in deep space. Use slow violet and blue lighting, then give me the film, audio master and editable show.”

Hour-long scores are supported. Render time depends on the resolution and machine; a full film may take longer than its playing time. The saved timeline makes revisions and alternate visual versions possible.
