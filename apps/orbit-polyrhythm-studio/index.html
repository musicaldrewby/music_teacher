1. Press Play. Your soundfont loads once (about 31 MB). Start with a preset, then make it yours.

2. Select a track. Click its name to edit. The sound button opens all 269 instruments and kits. M mutes; S solos. Multiple solos play together.

3. Shape the rhythm. Polyrhythm fits each track’s steps into the same cycle: 3 against 4 is three evenly spaced steps against four. Polymeter gives every step a sixteenth note, so different lengths drift past one another.

4. Make it melodic. Each step advances through your melody, including silent steps. Choose scale degrees, octave, single notes, chords or open fourths and fifths. Open intervals stay exactly five or seven semitones above each melody note. Drum kits have a dedicated drum-voice selector. Tap steps to cycle through a normal hit, accent and rest.

Composed phrases. Cosmic Marimba, Neon Night Drive, Gravity Games, Berlin School, Background Grooves and saved projects with composed phrases offer a measure selector above the rhythm grid. Choose a measure to edit its hits, pitches and note lengths. Enter note names such as D4 F4 A4, or a dash for a rest. Length is in beats; the Sound & space Note length control scales these written lengths. Key transposes the arrangement while retaining its chromatic notes. Scale and Play as stay fixed because these phrases contain individually written notes and chords. Other presets keep their original scale-degree editor.

5. Add space. Sound & space controls volume, pan, reverb, echo, note length and probability. Swell depth changes how softly or strongly each note is struck; Swell cycle sets a full soft–strong–soft wave in four-beat bars. Each track has an offset so layers trade prominence. Zero depth keeps the original steady velocities; accented steps remain stronger. These settings follow scenes and saved projects. Timbre motion adds slow filter, stereo and volume motion to a track; Motion cycle sets its pace in beats. Zero Timbre motion keeps the sound steady. Longer notes can overlap. Repeated pitches retrigger.

Living session. Open the panel above the Observatory to enable Keep Evolving. After about 20 seconds, parts begin subtle rhythmic variations, melodic turns and chord inversions using their existing notes. Variation sets how adventurous it gets; New path changes the repeatable sequence. Protect parts to preserve their written patterns. Groove & Feel adds swing, shuffle, laid-back timing or human touch. Every track’s cycle start stays on the grid. The editor shows the written base pattern; lights follow the performed notes. These choices are retained in scenes, projects, personal presets and audio exports. Existing Probability settings can still make live playback differ from an offline render.

Your collection. Star presets to find them under Favorites. Save as preset captures your current edits with a unique name and optional collection. My presets shows your saved arrangements. These are stored in this browser; Back up collection exports a portable JSON copy. Import merges a backup with your existing collection.

Ambient Reverb. Turn on the master effect below the editor for a spacious stereo tail. Decay spans 1–30 seconds; Mix blends dry and wet sound; Pre-delay separates the attack from the ambience. Tone, Width and Bass cut shape the space. Try Hall or Deep Space. Settings follow scenes and project saves. Stop lets the ambience ring out; Clear tail silences it. This adds to the individual tracks’ room reverb.

Render audio. Choose Render audio beside Save project to make a WAV or MP3 from the current preset, including your edits, effects and dynamics. Choose uncompressed WAV or high-quality 320 kbps MP3, then select 1–60 minutes. Both include gentle opening and closing fades. It renders offline with progress and cancellation. Long renders save directly to a file when supported. Browsers without direct file saving can download up to 10 minutes.

6. Capture an idea. Store the current setup into a scene, then recall it at the next shared-cycle boundary. Scenes include tempo and harmony. Save project downloads all four scenes and your current pattern. Edits are also remembered on this browser when storage is available.
