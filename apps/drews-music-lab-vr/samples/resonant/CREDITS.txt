Adaptations: mono, leading silence removed from gong, low-frequency noise filtering,
gentle noise reduction on gong, level adjustment, fading the recorded room noise
at the end, and MP3 encoding. Playback transposes the chime to the room's notes.
The source chime's dominant lowest tonal partial measures approximately 587.3 Hz
(D5); this measured frequency is used for tuning rather than the uploader's C4 tag.
No user attribution, personal information, voices, or outside links are recorded
into shared songs. Songs store only Music Lab instrument events.
