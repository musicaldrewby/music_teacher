MOVEMENT STOPLIGHT — NEW MOVEMENT SUITE

Nine movement pieces plus a one-shot Stop cue.
100 BPM, 4/4, G major. Movement loops: 4 measures / 9.6 seconds.
Each movement has its own melody, rhythm and orchestration; bass parts support the harmony independently.
Stop: one G6/9 vibraphone chord, 4.8-second file, no loop.
Set movement loops to bars 1–5; do not loop Stop. Load GeneralUser GS v1.471 with General MIDI programs.
Each instrument has a named MIDI track. MIDI contains notes, not recorded audio.
