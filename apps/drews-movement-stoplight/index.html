THE MOVEMENT COLLECTION

Nine moves. One shimmering stop.

Nine movement tracks at 100 BPM in G major, each with a 4-bar loop. Stop plays a single vibraphone chord and fades into silence. Listen here, or download the MIDI to edit the individual instrument parts in your music software. Hop adds a random jaw-harp boing on every beat. Slide adds an upward whistle at the start of measures 1 and 3. Music stops when you close this window.
