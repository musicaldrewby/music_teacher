Every app, in one place.

20 music apps by Drew Wichman. Each app opens from this GitHub website in a new tab.

Instruments

  • Mallet & Measure — Free Classroom Edition ↗

    Play eight classroom instruments, including soprano recorder, xylophones, glockenspiel, hand drum, wood block and triangle. Layer up to five tracks in four-measure loops, record your playing, edit notes and save your music.

  • Mr. Drew’s Auto Harp ↗

    Choose a chord, brush the strings, and record your performance. Explore six instrument sounds and four inviting rooms with this playable photographic autoharp.

  • Mr. Drew’s Uke Strum ↗

    Strum through songs with chord diagrams, lyrics, and classroom play-alongs.

  • Pocket Orff ↗

    A touch-friendly collection of xylophones, metallophones, and classroom percussion.

  • Interactive Xylophone ↗

    Play the bars, choose your pitches, and record, loop, or overdub a melody.

  • Mr. Drew’s Animated Vocal Explorations ↗

    Draw animated pitch paths and follow them with your voice, with themed scenes, looping, and optional sound.

Rhythm

  • Poison Rhythm ↗

    Outsmart the Musical Mastermind: echo the safe rhythms and stay silent when the poison rhythm returns. Customize your rhythms, tempo, stages, and classroom rounds.

  • Orbit — Polyrhythm Studio ↗

    Explore evolving ambient worlds and interlocking rhythms with eight instruments, deep reverb, 46 presets, and an immersive listening view.

  • Boomwhacker Play-Along — Teacher Edition ↗

    Create colorful arrangements with tubes, Orff instruments, percussion, and lyrics.

  • Rhythm Builder ↗

    Compose or generate rhythms, hear them, and save or print your favorites.

Games

  • Poison Rhythm ↗

    Outsmart the Musical Mastermind: echo the safe rhythms and stay silent when the poison rhythm returns. Customize your rhythms, tempo, stages, and classroom rounds.

  • Music Stoplight ↗

    Follow colorful musical signals to walk, jog, skip, gallop, and explore different ways to move.

  • Sneaky Cat ↗

    Take sneaky steps, freeze, and copy the cat’s solo or partner poses.

  • Mr. Drew’s Name That Tune! ↗

    Play a short song clip, take a guess, reveal the answer, and award team points.

  • Four in a Row ↗

    Choose your teams and turn classroom achievements into a friendly board game.

  • Mr. Drew’s Name That Note Challenge ↗

    Read treble and bass notes in arcade challenges or practice at your own pace.

  • From Tips to Tours ↗

    Start with a borrowed guitar and build an idle music career from bedroom practice to stadium shows, alien audiences, and interdimensional encores. Upgrade your instruments, grow your band, and keep earning while away.
