HOW IT WORKS

The seaside room fills the page. Its background repeats an 80-second cycle: 30 seconds at sunset, a 10-second fade to night, 30 seconds at night, and a 10-second fade back to sunset. The animation pause button freezes the cycle, and reduced-motion preferences keep the sunset still. The original sunset remains visible if the night image cannot load. A seagull visits the category rail during the day, and a small crab occasionally scuttles beside a cabinet, pauses, and walks completely off the left edge without fading out. If you scroll or switch cabinets, it continues from its current position to the edge. At night, a moth circles a warm light, shooting stars cross the sky, and a faint ship with tiny warm windows passes along the horizon. A sailboat can cross the water at either time of day, and an idle cabinet occasionally lights up. The seven occasional events each have their own randomized schedule, so small overlaps can occur naturally. Daytime and nighttime visitors keep their appropriate light setting. The first sailboat pass begins about 25–40 seconds into an active visit. All events follow the animation pause control, stop counting time while the page is hidden or a dialog is open, and are disabled by reduced-motion preferences. Fourteen illustrated cabinet designs are mixed through the collection. Every shelf, including category views, separates matching designs so no two neighboring cabinets look the same. The original order and each app's usual design are kept wherever possible; a different shell is used only when a future selection cannot be separated by reordering. Grand Theater appears on Vocal Explorations; Orbit appears on Rhythm Builder. Midnight Vector appears on Soundroom; Sunset Stripe appears on Four in a Row and Bingo Maker. Laser Lime appears on Name That Note, Indigo Wave on Felt & Fable Studio, and the glowing circuit cabinet with an MCP backdrop on Music Lab VR. Each cabinet opens its app's GPT Site in a new tab. Apps can appear in more than one category, including Teacher Tools. All Apps lists each app once. Category signs and arrows browse the collection; phones show one cabinet at a time and support swiping. The small information button shows details. Hearts save favorites in the visitor's browser. Search, a plain app directory, reduced motion, an animation pause control, and Buy Me a Coffee are included. Beach and seagull ambience is on by default on every fresh visit and starts automatically when the browser permits. Otherwise, it begins after the first click, tap, Enter, or Space. The speaker button mutes or resumes it for the current visit; a fresh page load starts with sound on again. It fades out and pauses while the page is hidden, then resumes when you return. The 94-second lossless loop uses an eight-second crossfade and gap-free Web Audio playback.

All artwork and fonts are included. No installation, build, backend, API key, or ChatGPT subscription is required to run this static homepage. The linked apps remain on their GPT Sites and follow those sites' availability and access settings.
