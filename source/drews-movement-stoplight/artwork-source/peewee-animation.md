# Animated Pee-wee bicycle

Generated with the built-in image_gen tool, then rigged with canvas-drawn trouser legs, shoes and pedals in scene-renderer.js. The base image is intentionally legless; the complete character is assembled during playback and video rendering.

Final asset: /workspace/sites/movement-stoplight/dist/assets/vehicles-v2/peewee-bicycle.png

## Base prompt

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: layered animation base sprite for a Saturday morning cartoon traffic game. Create ONE isolated right-facing SIDE VIEW of Pee-wee Herman (Paul Reubens's iconic character) riding his bright cherry-red vintage cruiser bicycle. Recognizable cheerful face, pale skin, short neatly slicked dark hair, gray fitted suit jacket and trousers at the hips, white shirt and red bow tie. Upright slightly eager posture, arms reaching forward and both hands holding curved chrome handlebars. Red bicycle frame with white trim, chrome headlight, rounded fenders, a black saddle and circular black tires with white sidewalls and silver spokes. Camera strict side elevation, wheels level, complete head and tires visible with transparent margin. Charming polished hand-painted cartoon with dark outlines and cel-shaded volume matching cartoon cars. IMPORTANT ANIMATION LAYER REQUIREMENT: OMIT BOTH OF THE RIDER'S LEGS AND SHOES BELOW THE HIPS COMPLETELY. End the rider neatly at the jacket hem/hip, seated on saddle. That region should reveal the bike behind him or actual transparency, because two gray trouser legs and black shoes will be animated and composited separately by the game engine. Keep the entire bicycle including its crank hub; do not draw separate floating parts or extra examples. Ordinary happy face on the human only, no faces on bicycle. Actual transparent background. No scenery, ground, cast shadow, labels, text, logos, other people, cars, fragments or watermark. Portrait/roughly square composition, high resolution.

## Crank removal edit

Precise object edit for an animation sprite. Preserve this exact Pee-wee Herman upper-body rider and red bicycle, same pixel framing, same dimensions and placement, same transparent background. Change ONLY this: remove the silver crank arm extending diagonally down-right from the central crank hub and remove the attached black pedal at its end, leaving actual transparency where they extended outside the chain guard. Keep the large circular silver crank hub at roughly (592,1009) intact, as a round axle with NO crank arm or pedal attached. The game will draw an animated crank and pedals. Keep both rider legs absent as they already are; the game supplies animated legs. Do not alter wheels, hands, head, suit, saddle, frame, fenders, chain, handlebars, or crop. Actual alpha transparency. No extra elements.
