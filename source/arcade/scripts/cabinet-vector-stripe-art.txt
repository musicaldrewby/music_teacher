APPROVED 1980s-INSPIRED CABINET ART

Midnight Vector and Sunset Stripe were generated as original illustrations,
previewed, and approved by Drew. The approved 1024 x 1536 transparent PNGs
were encoded as WebP with their dimensions and alpha channels preserved.

Midnight Vector — dist/assets/cabinet-midnight-vector.webp
Black upright cabinet with cyan trim, magenta controls, and original geometric
line art. Its empty teal marquee and rounded CRT receive live app content.
Used for Soundroom and Name That Note.

Sunset Stripe — dist/assets/cabinet-sunset-stripe.webp
Walnut and orange upright cabinet with original cream and amber ribbon art.
Its empty ivory marquee and rounded CRT receive live app content.
Used for Four in a Row and Bingo Maker.

The names above label the cabinet designs only. The webpage shows the actual
app titles on the marquees, fitted to at most two complete lines with padding.
All eleven designs remain represented in the sixteen-app collection.
