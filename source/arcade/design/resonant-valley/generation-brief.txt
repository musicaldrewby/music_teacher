Resonant Valley cabinet artwork

The arcade shell was generated with the built-in image generator using the existing From Tips to Tours cabinet as the front-view registration reference. It was exported at 1024 × 1536 with alpha. The shipping WebP was encoded at quality 84 with alpha quality 95. The live game title and playable screen are rendered by the arcade.

Production prompt

Edit the supplied front-view cabinet as a silhouette/registration template. Replace ALL of its guitar and band artwork with an entirely new physical arcade machine for the original peaceful voxel music sandbox RESONANT VALLEY. Deliver a 2:3 portrait standalone prop cutout with TRUE TRANSPARENCY around it, fully visible top and feet, thin margin, front-on camera (straight-on, shallow downward view only for joystick deck). Keep the same handsome wide set of feet, overall proportions, flared control deck halfway down, centered top strip and game screen. This cabinet must feel like the premium illustrated vintage machines of a warm wooden seaside arcade.

Art direct it as “the midnight river jukebox.” Enamelled deep INDIGO and midnight blue cabinet, subtle walnut edges, narrow luminous turquoise trim with a restrained muted lavender secondary accent, amber/brass details. The front kick panel is a beautifully composed bold original 16-bit pixel-mosaic inlay: a large, unmistakable lustrous golden concert HARP with visible tapering strings embedded in a hillside overlooking a winding bright cyan river of square-cut stone steps, nocturnal pixel pines, angular terraced cliffs, and a small gold square moon. Broad intentional shapes; harp must read at thumbnail size. A vinyl-record spindle detail or shallow dark record platter with copper label in the center of the control deck; one real turquoise ball joystick at left and three to six clear arcade buttons at right. Refined beveled wooden rails, visibly machined metal, soft dimensional studio highlights. Small decorative brass stepped fretwork, restrained cyan rim light. Omit lightning bolts, guitar picks, mirrored skyscapes, crowds, star fields, and anime.

The top marquee is a broad, flat, perfectly horizontal DARK INDIGO BLANK sign above the speakers for a later live title. The area of the sign available for live type is generous, centered, roughly x 20–80%, y 3.8–10.7% of the canvas. The second blank area is a big near-black landscape rectangular glass CRT screen, centered roughly x 20–80%, y 19–39%. NO titles, no words or letters anywhere, no numbers, no logos, no user interface and no image in the CRT. Both blank panels remain substantially empty and opaque, not alpha holes. Distinct small grille vents are fine. The front illustrated free standing cabinet fills the portrait image; present no environment, no floor, no backdrop, no stage, no detached shadows. Handcrafted game-prop illustration with rich reality of materials, clean crisp silhouette, not a flat vector drawing.
