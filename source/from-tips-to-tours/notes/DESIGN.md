# Agreed direction

- Rich SNES-inspired pixel art matching the approved From Tips to Tours concept.
- A genuine idle clicker, with automatic income and offline progress.
- Start with a poor beginner and a borrowed instrument; lessons and small gigs
  lead to a band, equipment, a van, road crew, tours, recordings and stardom.
- The Hollywood-hills home is a midpoint, not the ending.
- Continue into alien audiences, interstellar touring, impossible instruments,
  dimensional careers and cosmic absurdity.
- Target long-term play, with weeks/months of discoveries and replayable Encores.
- Musical ability is a progression theme; there are no required rhythm tests,
  notation quizzes or instrument performance exams.
- Songs are swappable placeholders. Future soundtracks may use synthesized
  music, sampled 16-bit-style instruments, or full replacement recordings.
- Do not require continual gig scheduling, bus dispatch or repetitive claiming.
- Eventually explore portal ad monetization, but no ad system is activated now.

## This first playable edition

48 career milestones across eight chapters; 24 upgradeable income sources;
eight gear/skill lines; 192 automatic source milestone boosts; 20 trophies;
two Encore scopes; browser save/backup/export/import; offline earnings reports;
multi-tab ownership; multiple scene atlases; animated musician and acquired band;
three original audio loops and a persistent, replaceable local music library.

Later content can expand each chapter without moving its save IDs. Add save
migrations when changing the shape of existing data. Preserve the approved
art direction and the independence of the soundtrack from progression.

## Implemented opening-career pass

The first improvement pass focuses on audible lessons, visible purchases and
Jules as the first bandmate. No cash multipliers, prices, venue gates, offline
caps or prestige formulas changed. Narrative discovery is automatic and its
reading is optional. The existing long-horizon simulations therefore remain
applicable to the numeric model.

Future passes can introduce career specialties, active-visit opportunities and
new Encore routes; those have not been implemented in this update.
